## 1. Install

Shadowrocket isn't in the China App Store. Buy it with a non-China Apple ID in another region's App Store (for example the US) — a one-off purchase of about US$3 (check the store page). Make sure the developer is **Shadow Launch Technology Limited**. No such Apple ID? See [Create a non-China Apple ID](/en/howto/apple-id/).

## 2. Import the subscription

1. Open Shadowrocket and tap **+** at the top right.
2. Set **Type** to **Subscribe**.
3. Paste the **Shadowrocket subscription URL** from the login-info file (not the Clash one) into **URL**; put your server's name in **Remark** if you like; tap **Done**.
4. `name-Reality` and `name-TLS` appear; tap `name-Reality` to select it.

## 3. Connect

1. Keep **Global Routing** on the home screen set to **Config** (rule-based) — not "Proxy", which sends every site abroad.
2. Turn on the connection switch at the top. The first time it asks to add a VPN configuration — tap "Allow" and confirm with Face ID or your passcode.

<Callout type="ok">
A VPN icon appears in the status bar; ip.net.coffee/claude/ in Safari shows your server's IP.
</Callout>

## Tips

- Tap the latency number next to a node to test it; pick one with normal latency.
- In the subscription settings you can turn on automatic updates (such as "update on open") so subscription changes sync automatically.

## Stuck?

<Q q="Nodes time out after importing?">
First check your network can reach the server: switch to mobile data or another Wi-Fi. Then try the other node (`name-TLS`).
</Q>
<Q q="I added the Clash subscription URL by mistake?">
Delete it and add the one labelled "Shadowrocket (iPhone)" in the login-info file.
</Q>
