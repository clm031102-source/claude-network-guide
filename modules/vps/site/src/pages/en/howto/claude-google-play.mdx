## Before you start

- **Google Play on your phone**: many phones sold in mainland China don't ship with Google services — install the Play Store and sign in to a Google account first (no account? see [Create a Google account](/en/howto/gmail/)).
- **A payment method on your Google account**: see [Add a payment card to your Google account](/en/howto/google-payment-card/), or top up with a Google Play gift card.
- **A Claude account**: you can sign up and log in right in the app with email or Google.

<Callout type="tip" title="In-app vs. web subscriptions">
Subscribe in the app and Google Play takes the payment and manages the subscription; the price may be higher than subscribing on claude.ai (the store takes a cut) — go by what the subscription screen shows. If you already subscribed on the web, don't subscribe again in the app, or you'll be charged twice.
</Callout>

Claude is only offered in countries and regions Anthropic supports; follow its terms when subscribing and using it.

## 1. Install and sign in

1. Open the Play Store, search for "**Claude by Anthropic**" (developer: Anthropic) and install it.
2. Open the app and log in with email or "Continue with Google".

## 2. Subscribe in the app

1. Tap your **initials** (top right) to open settings.
2. Tap "**Upgrade to Pro**" (or the upgrade option) and choose the plan and monthly or yearly.
3. The Google Play payment sheet appears; check the price, Google account and payment method, then tap Subscribe.

<Callout type="ok">
Settings shows your plan as Pro (or Max), and your Google account email gets a Google Play receipt.
</Callout>

## 3. Manage and cancel

Either works:

- **Claude app**: tap your initials → "**Billing**" → "**Manage subscription**".
- **Play Store**: tap your profile picture → "**Payments & subscriptions**" → "**Subscriptions**" → Claude.

Know that:

- **Cancel at least 24 hours before the next charge**, or it renews automatically;
- after cancelling you can keep using it until the period ends;
- a Google Play subscription is **managed only on Google's side** — you can't change it on claude.ai.

## Stuck?

<Q q="Can't find Claude in the Play Store, or &quot;not available in your country&quot;?">
The Play Store region follows your Google payments profile's country/region. Claude is only listed in supported regions, and the payments profile country can't simply be changed — see [Add a payment card to your Google account](/en/howto/google-payment-card/).
</Q>
<Q q="Payment declined?">
Check the card type is on Google Play's list and enabled for overseas payments; if it still fails, contact your card issuer or use a Google Play gift card.
</Q>
<Q q="I subscribed on the web but the app still says upgrade?">
Make sure the app is logged in to the same Claude account. Web and app subscriptions are separate — don't subscribe twice.
</Q>
