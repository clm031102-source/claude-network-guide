## What it is

A <Term k="DNS 泄露" text="DNS leak" />: when you visit a foreign site, its name is first looked up with Chinese DNS, letting test sites see your province and provider.

This setup's subscription already handles it: unlisted foreign sites aren't looked up locally — they're handed straight to the server.

## How to test

Open [ip.net.coffee/claude/](https://ip.net.coffee/claude/) and look at the "DNS leak test":

<Callout type="ok">
The DNS exit isn't in China (usually overseas DNS such as Cloudflare or Google).
</Callout>

## If Chinese DNS shows up

1. **Update the subscription**: tap "Update" on it in your app to get the latest rules (the setup fixed this in October 2026).
2. **Turn on Tun Mode** on computers, and check Clash Verge's **DNS Overwrite** is **off** (it replaces the subscription's DNS settings).
3. **Turn off other proxies**: other proxies or boosters on the device skew the result.
4. On Windows, with Tun Mode on, also turn on **Strict Route** so Windows can't bypass Clash for DNS.

<Callout type="tip">
Using Chinese DNS for Chinese sites is normal — that's for speed. The test is about foreign sites.
</Callout>
