## 1. Which installer to download

Open the official releases page [github.com/clash-verge-rev/clash-verge-rev/releases](https://github.com/clash-verge-rev/clash-verge-rev/releases) and, under the latest version's files, look for:

| Your Mac | Download |
|---|---|
| Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4…) | The file ending in `_aarch64.dmg` |
| Intel | The file ending in `_x64.dmg` |

Not sure: Apple menu at the top left → "About This Mac", see "Chip" or "Processor".

<Shot src="clash-verge-download.webp" w={1214} h={367} alt="The download section of the Clash Verge Rev release notes" caption="The release notes' download section (下载地址): on Mac pick Apple M芯片 (Apple silicon) or Intel芯片 (Intel)." />

## 2. Install

Double-click the dmg, drag Clash Verge into "Applications", then open it from "Applications".

## 3. Import the subscription

1. Open Clash Verge and click **Profiles** on the left.
2. Paste the **Clash subscription URL** from the login-info file into the "Profile URL" box and click **Import**.
3. Click the new profile card so it becomes selected.

## 4. Choose Rule mode

Click **Proxies** on the left and choose **Rule** at the top.

## 5. Turn on Tun Mode

1. Click **Settings** on the left and turn on **Tun Mode**. When it says the service is required, click **Install Service** and enter your Mac's login password.
2. In the Clash settings, keep **DNS Overwrite** off; set **IPv6** by whether your network has IPv6 — see [IPv6 on or off](/en/howto/clash-verge-tun/#ipv6-on-or-off).

Macs don't need Strict Route. More details: [Turn on TUN mode](/en/howto/clash-verge-tun/).

<Callout type="ok">
Home shows "TUN Mode Enabled"; ip.net.coffee/claude/ in your browser shows your server's IP.
</Callout>

## Stuck?

<Q q="It says the app is damaged or the developer can't be verified?">
Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, find the Clash Verge message near the bottom and click "Open Anyway". Still blocked? Run <Cmd c='sudo xattr -r -c "/Applications/Clash Verge.app"' /> in Terminal (enter your login password), then open it again.
</Q>
<Q q="Installing the service keeps failing?">
Make sure you have the latest version. Uninstall the old one and install the latest from the releases page.
</Q>
