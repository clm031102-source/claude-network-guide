## Before testing

- **Speed tests use a lot of data**: one run can be several to tens of GB — don't test often.
- **Test from where you are**: test on a phone or computer with the app connected — that's your real experience. Testing on the server itself (often several Gbps) only shows the server's exit is fast, not what you'll get.
- **Only this proxy on**: turn off other proxies and boosters.

## How

1. Connect the app and pick a node.
2. Open [fast.com](https://fast.com) and wait; click "Show more info" for latency and upload.
3. Test once in the daytime and **again between 8 and 11 pm** to compare.

## Reading the results

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Download speed | How fast video and downloads are. A few tens of Mbps is smooth for daily use |
| Latency | From China to the US West Coast, 100–250 ms is normal — distance decides it |
| Loaded latency | If latency jumps a lot while downloading, the route is congested |

## Fast by day, slow at night

Normal: routes from China abroad are most congested in the evening. BBR on the server helps a bit. If peak hours matter, choose a plan with a better route. See the Wiki: [Why it is slower in the evening](/en/wiki/peak-hours/).

<Callout type="tip">
Some test sites limit test length, and your router or Wi-Fi may be the bottleneck. Test over a cable for a more accurate result.
</Callout>
