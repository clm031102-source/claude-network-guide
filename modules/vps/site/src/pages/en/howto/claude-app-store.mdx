## Before you start

- **A non-China Apple ID**: the Claude app isn't on the China App Store. If you don't have one, see [Create a non-China Apple ID](/en/howto/apple-id/).
- **That Apple ID can pay**: Settings → your name → Payment & Shipping has a working payment method, or the account has a balance (from a gift card).
- **A Claude account**: you can sign up and log in right in the app with email or Google.

<Callout type="tip" title="In-app vs. web subscriptions">
Subscribe in the app and Apple takes the payment and manages the subscription; the price may be higher than subscribing on claude.ai (the store takes a cut) — go by what the subscription screen shows. If you already subscribed on the web, don't subscribe again in the app, or you'll be charged twice.
</Callout>

Claude is only offered in countries and regions Anthropic supports; follow its terms when subscribing and using it.

## 1. Download and sign in

1. Signed in to the App Store with your non-China Apple ID, search for "**Claude by Anthropic**" (developer: Anthropic) and install it.
2. Open the app and log in with email or "Continue with Google".

## 2. Subscribe in the app

1. Tap your **initials** (top right) to open settings.
2. Tap "**Upgrade to Pro**" (or the upgrade option) and choose the plan and monthly or yearly.
3. The App Store payment sheet appears; check the price and Apple ID, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your password.

<Callout type="ok">
Settings shows your plan as Pro (or Max), and your Apple ID email gets an App Store receipt.
</Callout>

## 3. Manage and cancel

Either works:

- **Claude app**: tap your initials → "**Billing**" → "**Manage subscription**".
- **iPhone Settings**: Settings → your name → "**Subscriptions**" → Claude.

Know that:

- **Cancel at least 24 hours before the next charge**, or it renews automatically;
- after cancelling you can keep using it until the period ends;
- an App Store subscription is **managed only on Apple's side** — you can't change it on claude.ai, and refunds go through Apple ([reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)).

## Stuck?

<Q q="Can't find Claude in the App Store?">
You're probably signed in with a China Apple ID. Sign out from your profile in the App Store, sign in with the non-China Apple ID and search again.
</Q>
<Q q="&quot;Cannot complete purchase&quot; or payment method invalid?">
Check Payment & Shipping for this Apple ID: the payment method must belong to its country/region, or there must be enough balance. Top up with that region's App Store gift card.
</Q>
<Q q="I subscribed on the web but the app still says upgrade?">
Make sure the app is logged in to the same Claude account. Web and app subscriptions are separate — don't subscribe twice.
</Q>
<Q q="Want to switch to a web subscription (possibly cheaper)?">
Cancel on Apple's side first; after the period ends, subscribe on claude.ai with a card.
</Q>
