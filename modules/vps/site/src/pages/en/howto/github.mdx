## Do you need it

**You can use this setup without an account.** The setup's repository and the release pages of Clash Verge Rev, FlClash and the rest are public.

You need an account to open issues in the setup's repository, star the project, or store your own code later.

## Steps

1. Open [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup).
2. Enter an email (Gmail recommended), set a password and choose a username (it becomes part of your profile URL).
3. Complete the human check and enter the code sent to your email.
4. After logging in, go to Settings → Password and authentication and turn on two-factor authentication.

<Callout type="ok">
You can log in, and your avatar at the top right opens your profile.
</Callout>

## How to report a problem

Open the [setup's Issues page](https://github.com/clm031102-source/claude-network-guide/issues), click New issue and say: which step you reached, the exact message you saw (paste the text; blur IPs, subscription URLs and passwords in screenshots), and what you've tried.

<Callout type="no">
Never include your server IP, passwords, subscription URLs or keys in an issue.
</Callout>
